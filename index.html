Who We Are

Eden Christian Community (ECC) has a divine mandate to reach the world with wholesome, entertaining, and spiritually-edifying programmes and contents.

Eden Christian Community is duly registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), Nigeria.

Our Ministries

Our Arms

ECC expresses her mandate to the world through three key Directorates, each dedicated to producing and distributing life-changing content.

The organisation has three Directorates through which her mandate to the world is expressed:

Eden Pictures

ECC's visual media directorate — producing and distributing inspirational films, visual content, and entertainment that transforms lives and glorifies God.

Eden Sounds

The music and audio arm of ECC — creating, recording, and promoting anointed gospel music and audio content that ministers to the church and beyond.

Family Hub

A dedicated space for families — providing biblical resources, counselling, enrichment programmes, and community support to strengthen homes in Christ.

Our Programs

Intentional programmes designed to strengthen couples, families, and believers in their walk with God.

Monthly Virtual Couples Hangout

A fun, faith-filled online gathering for couples to connect, share, laugh, and grow together in Christ. Held every month, this virtual experience brings married couples across our community into one space for encouragement, teaching, and fellowship.

Monthly · Virtual / Online

Couples Getaway

An annual retreat exclusively for couples — a time to unplug, reconnect, and reignite the flame of love and purpose. Set in a refreshing environment, the Couples Getaway combines Spirit-led sessions, relaxation, and intimate moments designed to strengthen marriages.
